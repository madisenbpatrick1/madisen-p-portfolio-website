**Summary:**  
I’m building a todo app that does one thing beautifully, help you focus. No clutter, no sign-ups, no distractions. Just a clean space to write, act, and move on.  

---

## ✅ *Why I’m Building It*  

I’ve used them all — **Todoist, Notion, Things, Apple Reminders** — polished, powerful, and ultimately overwhelming.  
Somewhere between all the color-coding and automation, I missed the simplicity of **pen and paper**.  

So I’m creating something smaller, quieter, simpler; a minimalist todo app.  
Not to launch a company, but to practice the craft of building:  
- 🧠 Deepen my **Xcode** skills  
- 🏗️ Understand app **architecture** end-to-end  
- 🎨 Explore the full journey from **idea → design → development**

---

## 🧩 Learning Through Building  

I learn by doing.  

This project takes me through the full cycle:  
1. Define what truly matters  
2. Design it simply  
3. Build it cleanly  
4. Refine until it feels effortless  

By working through every stage myself, I’m learning how **design and logic** align to create something that just works.  

---

## ✨ The Vision  

A todo app with one goal, *clarity.*  

No accounts.  
No themes.  
No extra buttons.  

Just open → add → check → done.  

It’s designed like a **digital notebook** calm, focused, and intentional.  
Whitespace and typography replace clutter and noise.  

---

## 🛠️ The Process  

Every step teaches something new:  
- ⚙️ Experimenting with **SwiftUI**  
- 🔁 Managing **state and structure**  
- 🧭 Balancing **simplicity and usability**  

I’m documenting the full process from sketches to code to share as a **blog series and case study** later.   

---

## 🌿 What Minimalism Teaches  

Minimalism isn’t about less.  
It’s about **purpose**.  

Every feature earns its place. Every decision has intention.  

> Simplicity creates focus — in design, in work, and in life.
